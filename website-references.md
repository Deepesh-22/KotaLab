# Reference sites for your website-selling business (Kota, Rajasthan)

Goal: a business site that (a) proves you can make premium, animated websites, and (b) still converts a Kota/Indore/Jaipur business owner who is browsing on a ₹12–15k Android phone on 4G.

Those two goals fight each other, so the list below is split by **how realistic each reference is for a small team** — not by how fancy it looks.

---

## 0. The short answer: steal a blend of these 3, not one

| Take from | What exactly | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Lusion** — `lusion.co` | The *motion standard*: one hero 3D scene, scroll-scrubbed reveals, project tiles tagged by discipline, visible media/scroll affordances | This is the most "premium award-winning" bar you can aim at with Three.js, and it's a *studio selling 3D work* — exactly your pitch |
| **Dogstudio** — `dogstudio.co` | One single 3D object (their dog) that stays on screen the whole scroll, changes colour per project | Highest impact-per-rupee build: **one** 3D asset + GSAP timeline, not 12 scenes. Closest to something your team can actually ship |
| **Hubtown by Unseen Studio** (`unseen.co`) | Corporate/B2B 3D done tastefully — a 3D "monolith" hero for an Indian real-estate brand, Awwwards SOTD | This is what your *clients* secretly want (a boring business made to look big). Also proves an Indian brand can look world-class |

If you build those three ideas into one site — **dark, one hero 3D element, buttery scroll, real case studies** — you'll be ahead of 95% of agencies in Rajasthan.

## 2. Tier B — Realistic for you to actually build (2–6 weeks)

These use GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis + **one** Three.js hero. This is your lane.

1. **dogstudio.co** — single 3D object + colour-changing scroll. *The #1 reference for your homepage.*
2. **locomotive.ca** — the studio that wrote the smooth-scroll library (Lenis). Shows the ceiling of pure 2D scroll craft: masked image reveals, split-text headlines, magnetic cursor. No heavy 3D needed.
3. **darkroom.engineering** — Next.js + Lenis + GSAP; full-bleed project images scrolling past pixel typography. Cheap to build, looks expensive.
4. **obys.agency** — typography-driven, huge kinetic type, grid breaks. Great if your 3D skills are still growing: **type + motion can look more premium than mediocre 3D.**
5. **unseen.co** — art direction + engineering; the site that made the Hubtown 3D monolith.
6. **studiomesmer.com** / **pixeto.co** / **creativeapes.design** (all India) — slightly less extreme versions of the same language; good proof that this style wins awards from India too.
7. **lucerra.co**, **trionn.com** (India) — agency sites you can realistically be compared against when pitching Indian clients.

**Recommendation:** pick *Dogstudio* for the hero behaviour, *Locomotive* for scroll typography, *Basement* for copy structure, *Hubtown/Unseen* for the "we make real businesses look premium" tone.

## 3. If you want to launch in ~2 weeks instead of 2 months

- **Spline** (`spline.design`) — design the 3D hero in a browser, export/embed. Gets you 80% of the Dogstudio look without writing shaders.
- **ReactBits Agency template** (`pro.reactbits.dev/docs/templates/agency-site`) — Next.js + R3F + GSAP + Lenis, WebGL wave-shader hero, pinned sections, sticky footer reveal. Closest thing to a ready-made version of what you want.
- **nextjstemplates.com/templates/agency** — Next.js agency/portfolio starter (clean, easy to reskin).
- **Framer / Webflow templates** — search "3D agency" / "WebGL studio". Fastest path to a good-looking site with zero maintenance; you can still sell custom builds on the side.

Starting from a template is not a weakness — you're selling websites, and your own site is the demo. Ship it, then upgrade the 3D as you learn.

Non-negotiable for your market: **a floating WhatsApp button on mobile**, click-to-call, pricing page, and a Google Business Profile + "website design in Kota" SEO landing pages. The award-winning look wins the *pitch*; WhatsApp and pricing win the *sale*.

## 5. Real-world constraints (this is where most Indian agencies break their own site)

- **Mobile-first or bust.** ~80% of Kota traffic will be Android on 4G. Serve a **static image or short video instead of the WebGL scene** below `768px` or on low-end devices. Your site must stay usable and fast on a ₹10k phone — that's also the best sales argument you have ("see how fast this loads? we'll do that for you").
- **Load the 3D scene lazily**, after the hero text paints. Keep LCP under ~2.5s.
- **No scroll-jacking.** Smooth scroll (Lenis) is fine; hijacking is not. Local B2B clients hate it and mobile users abandon.
- **Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`** — and put it in your pitch deck as a checkbox competitors don't tick.
- **Hindi/Hinglish option.** A language toggle on the *sales* pages (not the fancy hero) is a genuine differentiator in Kota.
- **Case study truthfulness.** Weak case studies with inflated numbers read instantly fake. Three honest ones with real numbers beat ten pretty screenshots.

---

## 6. Recommended stack (the ladder, easiest → hardest)

1. **Easiest:** Framer / Webflow + Spline embed → live in days, no 3D maths.
2. **Sweet spot:** Next.js + GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis + **one** React Three Fiber scene (drei helpers, no custom shaders). This is the stack 90% of Tier-B sites above use.
3. **Advanced:** custom GLSL shaders, Theatre.js for timeline choreography, Blender for the hero model, draco-compressed GLB < 2 MB.
4. **Only if you're going full Active Theory:** your own render pipeline, WebGPU, particle systems. Not needed for year one.

---

## 7. What to avoid

- Copying Lusion/Active Theory 1:1 — it looks like a template to anyone who knows the space, and it will be too heavy for your clients' phones.
- 3D that requires a tutorial to navigate. Every 3D flourish must have a non-3D fallback.
- A "portfolio" of screenshots. Case studies with outcomes are what make a Kota business owner pay ₹40k instead of ₹8k.
- Long loading intro/loader animations on the *inner* pages. Keep the drama for the homepage only.
- No prices, no phone number, no reviews above the fold on mobile.

---

## TL;DR — open these five, in this order

1. `dogstudio.co` — how to make **one** 3D thing carry a whole site
2. `lusion.co` — the motion ceiling, and how to tag/cluster work
3. `locomotive.ca` — scroll craft without heavy 3D
4. `basement.studio` — how the words sell
5. `unseen.co` — proof an Indian business site can look world-class (Hubtown)

Then build: hero 3D → work grid → 3 honest case studies → pricing → WhatsApp. Ship in 4 weeks, upgrade the 3D later.
