Yes, we offer the fastest website delivery in Kota without using generic templates. A 5-page Launch website goes live in just 5–7 days, a full 10-page Business website launches in 10–14 days (1–2 weeks), and custom e-commerce or interactive 3D WebGL websites ship in 2–4 weeks.

03 What types of businesses in Kota do you build websites for?

We design and build websites for coaching institutes, student hostels and PGs, clinics and hospitals, optical and retail showrooms, restaurants, hotels, startups, and local service businesses across Kota — including Rajeev Gandhi Nagar, Talwandi, Vigyan Nagar, Jawahar Nagar, Kunhari, Landmark City, Gumanpura, and Borkhera — as well as clients across India.

04 Will my website rank on Google and load fast on budget 4G phones?

Yes. Every KotaLabs site is hand-coded without bloated page-builder plugins, uses compressed WebP imagery and self-hosted fonts, and is tested on budget Android phones over 4G. We also configure semantic headings, structured data, XML sitemaps, Google Search Console, and your Google Business Profile to help local customers find you on Google Search and Maps.

05 Do I own my website code and domain, or is there a monthly lock-in?

You own 100% of your source code, project files, and domain from day one with zero lock-in. Pricing is one-time for design and build, plus 30–60 days of post-launch founder support on WhatsApp. Ongoing monthly care is completely optional.

06 Can we launch a website without buying a custom domain immediately?

Yes. You can launch immediately on fast, SSL-secured cloud hosting with zero domain cost and still get indexed on Google Search and Google Maps. Whenever you decide to buy a .in or .com domain later, we connect it and set up permanent 301 redirects so you keep all your links and search rankings.
